# Cars
## Movie Overview
### Genre: Animation, Sports Comedy, & Drama

**Cars** tells the story of *Lightning McQueen*, a hotshot rookie race car driven to succeed. On his way to the biggest race of his life, he unexpectedly gets stranded in *Radiator Springs*, a sleepy town along Route 66, where he learns what truly matters in life.

> "Ah, it's just an old cup." — Lightning McQueen  
> "It's just an empty cup." — Doc Hudson

1. Piston Cup Tiebreaker: McQueen's race against The King and Chick Hicks
2. Radiator Springs Detour: Finding the forgotten heart of Route 66
3. The Final Race: A triumph of sportsmanship over victory

- Lightning McQueen
- Doc Hudson
- Mater
- Sally Carrera

Lightning McQueen's iconic racing number is `95`.
